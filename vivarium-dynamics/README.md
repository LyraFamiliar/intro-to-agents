# Vivarium Dynamics™

*A Division of Vivarium Dynamics Biological Holdings · Est. 1993*

A fictional biotech company that sells genetically authored creatures which
hatch from eggs — marketed with the sincerity and overpromise of 1990s
mail-order Sea Monkeys.

**Tagline:** Life, individually authored.

## The thesis

> We declared war on chance, and we lose every single time — in public, one
> Specimen at a time.

Vivarium industrialised the genetic lottery to eliminate randomness. Every egg
proves they can't. Rarity isn't a product they sell; it's the enemy winning,
documented honestly in the Registry.

Kids read a lab that adores its animals. Adults read Icarus with a pegboard SKU.
Same copy, both true, nobody winks.
