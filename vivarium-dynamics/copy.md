# Copy

## Master paragraph

Approved verbatim for cartons, inserts, and the Registry enrollment card.

> At Vivarium Dynamics, we believe no living thing should be left to chance.
> Since 1993, our laboratories have worked toward a single goal: a creature
> authored with intention — its color, its character, its every small habit
> chosen with care, so that nothing about it is accidental. Your Specimen
> arrives as an egg, dormant and complete, carrying a genetic signature that has
> never existed before and will never exist again. Raise it well. Record what it
> becomes. Report what surprises you. We are still learning, and every Specimen
> teaches us something we did not intend to know.

"Report what surprises you" is the Recovery Program hiding in plain sight — a
kid reads an invitation, an adult reads surveillance.

## Voice

Institutional sincerity. A research organisation writing to someone it trusts
with a living thing: warm, precise, faintly formal, never cute. Specimens are
*observed* and *recorded*, never "played with." Dormancy is a *phase.*

**Correct**
- "Your Specimen is unlike any Specimen in recorded history."
- "Dormancy is a natural phase of the Specimen lifecycle."
- "Raise it well. Record what it becomes."

**Prohibited**
- Any acknowledgement that the Specimen is small, simple, or disappointing
- Humour at the Specimen's expense, or at our own
- The words *die, dead, death, pet, toy, gimmick*
- Exclamation marks in Registry correspondence

## Nomenclature

*Specimen* is always capitalised, always singular when addressing an owner.
*Registry* and *Legacy Services* carry the trademark on first use per surface.
Never abbreviate the company to VD in customer-facing copy.
