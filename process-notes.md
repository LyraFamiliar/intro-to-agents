# Process notes

## Exercise 1 — branding with an agent as consultant

**Method:** instead of asking for a brand, I asked the agent to act as a brand
consultant and *interview me* — specifically to ask questions I wouldn't know to
ask. Four rounds of multiple-choice questions, then synthesis.

**What worked:**

- **Constraint questions before creative ones.** The first round was entirely
  about commercial and ethical constraints (who pays, does the creature die, is
  it a period piece) — not logo or name. Every later creative decision fell out
  of those four answers.
- **Being told when my answers conflicted.** Twice the agent flagged a genuine
  contradiction rather than papering over it: sincere-overpromise vs. knowing
  90s-costume, and selling a finite-lifespan creature to children. Both needed
  resolving before anything visual could be designed.
- **Being told what I'd accidentally built.** I picked randomized rarity, tiered
  scarcity, a collector registry, and a kid-facing retail SKU — which together
  are a loot box aimed at minors. I did not see that. Later, the $4.99 egg plus
  the $200 anomaly buy-back rebuilt the same problem through a side door.
- **Letting my throwaway joke become the thesis.** The taxidermy upsell was a
  one-line aside; the agent treated it as the strongest idea in the session and
  built the whole Legacy Services ladder on it.

**What to do differently:**

- I answered "keep it light, 5 minutes" mid-exercise and then kept going for an
  hour. Worth deciding the scope up front.
- The interview format (multiple choice with tradeoffs stated per option) was
  much faster than open questions. Ask for that explicitly next time.

**Transcript note:** this repo holds the decisions and outputs, reconstructed
from the session — not a verbatim chat log.
